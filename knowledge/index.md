# GeekyAnts Knowledge Base

Canonical reference for GeekyAnts company facts, services, technology, products,
and the `create-ai-native-project` project. Structured as an **Open Knowledge
Format (OKF) v0.1** bundle — a tree of markdown concept files, each with YAML
frontmatter, for AI-agent and human consumption.

- [Organization](./organization/index.md) — Company profile, leadership, and offices
- [Services](./services/index.md) — Consulting, engineering, and engagement models
- [Technology](./technology/index.md) — Supported technology stack
- [Products](./products/index.md) — Products and open source
- [Resources](./resources/index.md) — External content, company, and social links
- [Project](./project/index.md) — The `create-ai-native-project` CLI: overview, decisions, registry, layout, and change log
